PVCollada validation and support resources
==========================================

The COLLADA XSD, PVCollada XSD, and three PVCollada Schematron files in this
directory are copied without modification from https://github.com/pvlib/pvcollada at commit
2fcb4616f77ea26c875a758d11f7061fd41a2250.

The exact upstream PVCollada BSD 2-Clause LICENSE is included as
LICENSE.pvcollada.txt.

The COLLADA XSD imports the W3C XML namespace schema and the optional MathML
formula namespace by remote URL. xml.xsd is separately sourced from W3C.
mathml2_geometry_stub.xsd is a Heliotelligence-created local support resource
that prevents runtime network resolution of the optional MathML schema. S3
does not interpret COLLADA formula or kinematics payloads; formula content
remains outside the supported geometry contract.
